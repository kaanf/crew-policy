# Delete Your Crew Account

Last updated: August 2026

App: Crew – Pub Game Nights · Developer: Kaan Fırat

This page explains how to delete your Crew account, what data is removed, and how long deletion takes.

## Option 1: Delete Your Account in the App

1. Open the Crew app and sign in.
2. Go to Profile → Settings → Delete Account.
3. Confirm the deletion. Your account and associated data will be removed.

## Option 2: Request Deletion by Email

If you can no longer access the app, email us at frtpkaan@gmail.com from the email address linked to your Crew account, with the subject "Account Deletion Request". We will verify the request and delete your account within 30 days.

## What Gets Deleted

- Your account (name, email address, login credentials)
- Your profile photo and any photos taken during game missions
- Your game activity (matches, points, challenge history)

## Data Retention

All personal data listed above is permanently deleted within 30 days of a verified request. Anonymized, aggregated statistics that can no longer be linked to you (for example, total participants at an event) may be retained.
